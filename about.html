About · परिचय

Lakshya (लक्ष्य) is a free daily puzzle: six numbers, four operations (+ − × ÷), one target. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day. Fewer moves, more stars. It works offline once opened and can be added to your home screen.

लक्ष्य एक मुफ़्त रोज़ की पहेली है: छह संख्याएँ, चार क्रियाएँ, एक लक्ष्य। हर किसी को रोज़ वही पहेली मिलती है। कम चालें, ज़्यादा सितारे।

Rules in short

Tap a number, an operation, then another number. Results must be positive whole numbers. Reach the target in par moves for 3 stars; a hint caps you at 2. One missed day a week does not break your streak. Practice is unlimited and does not change your streak.

No money, no stakes

No purchases, no prizes, no betting. Original puzzles, art and text.
